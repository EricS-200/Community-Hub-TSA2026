# Electrical inspection

This checklist is for **visual preparation and supervised team review**, not a replacement for official inspection criteria or electrical safety instruction.

{% hint style="danger" %}
De-energize equipment before touching connections. Battery, high-current, and energized troubleshooting must follow your program's safety rules and qualified supervision.
{% endhint %}

## Pre-power visual checks

* [ ] Components are securely mounted and protected against movement.
* [ ] Wires are routed away from sharp edges, moving parts, and pinch points.
* [ ] Connections and ports are labeled where useful.
* [ ] Connectors have appropriate strain relief.
* [ ] No visibly damaged insulation or exposed conductors.
* [ ] Motors, controllers, and sensors match the team's current wiring diagram.
* [ ] A responsible reviewer confirms compliance with current program and vendor instructions.

## After supervised power-up

Observe status indicators, logs, sensor readings, and commanded behavior from a safe position. If something is unexpected, stop and investigate through the approved procedure.

**Record:** date, subsystem, inspector, observed issue, corrective action, and retest result.

Read: [FRC program resources](https://www.firstinspires.org/robotics/frc), [FTC Docs](https://ftc-docs.firstinspires.org/), and [REV Docs](https://docs.revrobotics.com/).
