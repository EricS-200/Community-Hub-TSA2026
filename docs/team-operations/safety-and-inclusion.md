# Safety & inclusion

Technical performance matters, but never at the cost of safe participation or respectful team culture.

## Non-negotiable habits

* Follow official FIRST safety guidance, event policies, and relevant tool/manufacturer instructions.
* Train students before giving access to hazardous tools or tasks.
* Keep a clear stop-work process when anyone notices a hazard.
* Assign appropriate adult supervision for activities that require it.
* Provide ways to report concerns without retaliation.
* Consider physical accessibility, cost barriers, schedules, and different prior experiences when planning participation.

{% hint style="danger" %}
This page is general guidance, not a substitute for formal safety training, team safeguarding policies, or the current FIRST manuals.
{% endhint %}

## Practice inclusion

A student should be able to contribute meaningfully without already knowing a programming language, having access to expensive tools at home, or being comfortable speaking first in every meeting.

Rotate opportunities, pair experienced members with beginners, and use clear written task definitions.

Read the current [FIRST program guidance](https://www.firstinspires.org/) and adopt policies appropriate to your organization.
