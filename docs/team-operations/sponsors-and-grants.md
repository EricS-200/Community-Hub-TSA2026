# Sponsors & grants

Funding works best when you explain what a contribution enables and follow up with evidence of the impact.

## A strong sponsor proposal

Keep the first message brief. Include:

1. Who your team serves and what students do.
2. A concrete need (for example, travel support, tools, materials, or access programs).
3. The amount or kind of help requested.
4. How the support will benefit students or the wider community.
5. A realistic follow-up commitment.

## Sample outreach structure

> **Subject:** Support hands-on robotics education in our community
>
> Hello \[name], our student robotics team helps young people learn engineering, programming, and collaborative problem-solving through FIRST. We're seeking support for \[specific need] this season. A contribution of \[amount or in-kind support] would help us \[measurable outcome]. May we send a one-page overview or schedule a short conversation?

Adapt the message to your real team, finances, and audience. Do not promise benefits or tax treatment your organization cannot provide.

## Track the pipeline

| Prospect                  | Alignment              | Ask                 | Contact owner | Next step            |
| ------------------------- | ---------------------- | ------------------- | ------------- | -------------------- |
| Local engineering company | Mentorship / materials | Define need         | Assign        | Research             |
| Community foundation      | Student access         | Check guidelines    | Assign        | Review eligibility   |
| Manufacturer              | Parts / fabrication    | List specifications | Assign        | Request conversation |

## Grant readiness checklist

* [ ] Confirm applicant eligibility and nonprofit/fiscal-sponsor requirements
* [ ] Read the current grant terms and deadlines
* [ ] Build a justified budget
* [ ] Define measurable outcomes and reporting capacity
* [ ] Collect required letters, financial documents, and approvals
* [ ] Plan a truthful impact report

Find program-level opportunities through [FIRST](https://www.firstinspires.org/) and research local community foundations. **Do not assume a grant is open or that a team qualifies without checking the current application.**

## Use existing source material

Start with [the funding collection](../resource-library/outreach-sponsors-grants.md) and [KY FIRST grants](https://www.kyfirstrobotics.org/team-grants). Keep source URL, date checked, applicant requirements, budget owner and reporting deadline in the tracker. Treat an old successful application as an example of structure, not proof of current eligibility.
